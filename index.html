Questions

Good to know

How do I place an order?

Message us on WhatsApp on 0810 610 5523 or use the order form on this page. Those are the only two ways we accept orders.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

Yes. We deliver nationwide across Nigeria. Delivery time and fee depend on the state, and we'll confirm both before you pay.

How far ahead should I order?

For same-day delivery in major cities, order as early as you can. For other states and big occasions like Valentine's or Christmas, order at least 2 to 3 days ahead.

Can I stay anonymous?

Of course. Tell us and we'll deliver without revealing who it's from. That's the whole point of a surprise.

How do I pay?

We'll send you the payment details on WhatsApp once your order is confirmed. Your gift is prepared after payment is received.
